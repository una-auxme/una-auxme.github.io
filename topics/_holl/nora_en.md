**What is it about?**

In NORA, cobots learn workflows from human demonstrations, recorded via video, speech and motion capture, instead of being programmed in the classical way. To achieve this, we adapt Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) using real and simulated datasets and run them on a UR cobot.

**Your tasks**

- Create real-world datasets: motion capture, data studies with participants, and annotation
- Create virtual datasets in NVIDIA Isaac Sim
- Work with VLAs: fine-tuning and verifying the results in simulation and on the robot
- Develop pipelines that connect VLAs with cameras, speech and robot control and run them on the UR cobot

**Your profile**

- Studies in computer science, mechatronics, robotics or a related field
- Good Python skills, first experience with PyTorch
- Careful and independent way of working
- Very good German and good English skills, written and spoken
- Interest in long-term collaboration
- A plus: experience with LeRobot, ROS 2, Isaac Sim or motion capture

**What we offer**

- Research on AI and robotics with a UR cobot, motion capture and GPU resources
- Flexible working hours and close supervision
- Opportunity to write a Bachelor's or Master's thesis within the project
